5.2 When a reward is credited

  • A reward is credited after the Survey Partner confirms it through a server-side callback. A page saying "complete" does not count.

5.3 How the amount is calculated

We convert the US dollar amount the partner actually pays us, using the agreed share and the live exchange rate. The amount shown on the survey wall already reflects this conversion, so what you see on the wall is what gets credited, apart from small differences caused by rate movement.

5.4 Screenout compensation

Some surveys pay a small amount even when you are screened out. Whether they do, and how much, is decided by the partner, and we credit whatever is returned. No compensation is also normal and is not a breach by the Site.

6. Reversals and clawbacks

In short: if a partner rejects a survey afterwards for quality reasons, the money is taken back. That is the partner's decision, not ours.

Survey Partners may audit answer quality after the fact and reverse a reward when they judge it invalid. Rewards already paid out may be clawed back in the following situations:

  • Abnormal response times (too fast, erratic switching, machine-like pacing).
  • Contradictory answers, obvious random clicking, or straight-lining.
  • Failing the partner's quality checks or attention-check questions.
  • Repeating the same study, or participating with ineligible details.
  • The partner classifying the sample as fraudulent traffic.

A reversal deducts the corresponding amount from your current balance. If the balance is insufficient it may go negative, and future rewards are applied against the deficit first. Every reversal is marked in your ledger, so you can check it against your records.

If you believe a reversal is wrong, open a ticket. We will take it up with the partner, but the final determination rests with them.
