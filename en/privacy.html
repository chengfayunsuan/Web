1. About this policy

In short: this policy covers everything you do on chengfa.org. Processing that happens on a Survey Partner's own survey page is governed by that partner's privacy policy, not this one.

1.1 Scope

This policy applies to all interaction under the chengfa.org domain and its subdomains, including registration, sign-in, browsing the survey hall, opening a survey, viewing your balance, submitting a support ticket, and requesting a withdrawal or redemption.

1.2 What this policy does not cover

Surveys run on the Survey Partner's servers. Once you open a survey, the answers you provide there, the cookies and tracking technologies on that page, and that page's access to your device are processed by the partner as an independent data controller under its own privacy policy. We neither control nor take responsibility for that processing. What we can guarantee is how we handle your data before you leave this Site.

7. Automated decisions and anti-fraud

In short: we automatically assess whether you are a proxy or a script. The result may block you from a survey or suspend your account. You can request human review.

7.1 What is assessed

  • Whether multiple accounts were registered from the same network within a short period.
  • Whether your exit IP belongs to a proxy, VPN or datacenter network.
  • Whether survey click rates are abnormal, far above human speed.
  • Whether participation records and device identifiers show scripted patterns.

7.2 Consequences

  • Refusal of participation in a survey: no points deducted, no effect on the account; you simply cannot enter that survey this time.
  • Account suspension: in serious cases, such as confirmed proxy-based farming, the account is suspended and 8 experience points are deducted. Suspensions are visible in Settings.

7.3 Your safeguards

Under Article 22 of the GDPR you have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, and the right to obtain human intervention. If you believe a decision was wrong, open a support ticket and request human review. We will give a conclusion within 7 business days. You may attach an explanation or evidence, such as a description of your network environment.

Automated assessment uses only IP network attributes, request frequency, device identifiers and account activity. It is never based on race, ethnicity, religion, political opinion, health, sexual orientation or trade-union membership, and it does not produce a credit score.

10. Security

In short: HTTPS everywhere, passwords stored only as hashes, database never exposed to the browser, separate authentication for administration. No internet service can promise absolute security.

  • Transport: HTTPS is enforced site-wide; data between browser and server is encrypted.
  • Passwords: stored only as salted, irreversible hashes. Nobody, including the administrator, can recover the original.
  • Sessions: the sign-in credential lives in an HttpOnly cookie that page scripts cannot read, reducing exposure to XSS.
  • Database: access is confined to server-side functions; connection credentials are never sent to the browser.
  • Privilege: the administration area uses separate authentication; administrative actions are logged.
  • Minimisation: we collect only what the service needs, and nothing unrelated to the business.

Breach notification: if a data breach occurs that may affect your rights, we will notify you as soon as reasonably possible through an in-site message or email, describing the scope, the likely impact and the measures taken. Where we are legally required to report to a supervisory authority, we will do so.

Please also protect your side: use a unique password that you do not reuse elsewhere, do not share your account, and contact us if you notice unusual sign-in activity.
