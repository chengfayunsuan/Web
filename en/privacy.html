PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Chengfa ("we", "us", "the Site") handles when you register and take part in surveys, the lawful basis for that processing, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and what rights you have.

1. What this site does

Chengfa is an entry point for paid market research. You register an account, choose an open survey from the survey hall, and earn rewards for completing it. Rewards can be withdrawn or redeemed for digital goods. The surveys themselves are provided and operated by third-party research companies ("Survey Partners"). We operate the entry point, the account system, and the settlement.

2. Who is the data controller

The data controller is Chengfa, operated by an individual established in Kaifeng, Henan, China. Contact: support@chengfa.org. We respond to privacy, access, deletion and complaint requests at this address within 30 days.

We do not have an establishment in the European Union. If you are located in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we handle your GDPR or UK GDPR requests through the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

3. What we collect

3.1 Registration and sign-in

  • Email address: used as your login credential and the only way to recover your account.
  • Username: displayed on the Site and editable at any time in Settings.
  • Password: stored only as an irreversible salted hash. Nobody, including the administrator, can recover the original password.
  • Registration and sign-in records: IP address, network attribution (carrier and region), browser identifier, last login time. Used to detect abnormal logins and prevent bulk registration.

3.2 Taking part in surveys

  • Which survey you opened, when, and which Survey Partner provided it.
  • Optional survey profile: age, gender, postal code. Stored only if you choose to provide it, and used for sample matching with Survey Partners. Providing it is not required to use the Site.
  • Your actual survey answers are collected directly by the Survey Partner's page and do not pass through our servers. We store only the record that you participated in a given survey, so that we can prevent duplicate participation and reconcile rewards.
  • Results returned by Survey Partners: complete, screenout, quota full, rejected or reversed, together with the corresponding reward amount.

3.3 Balances and redemptions

  • Balance, held amount, and every ledger entry with its source.
  • Withdrawal requests: amount, channel, and the payout account you provide.
  • Redemption orders: item, amount, and the issued code or reference number.

3.4 Communications

  • Support tickets you submit, in-site messages you send, and notices we send you.

3.5 Security checks

  • Cloudflare Turnstile is used at registration and sign-in to distinguish humans from automated requests. It processes your IP address and browser signals. We receive only a pass/fail result and do not store the raw verification data.
  • Network attribution: when you open a page, your exit IP is checked once for network properties (proxy, VPN, datacenter) and the result is cached for 24 hours. The lookup is performed by ip-api.com and transmits only the IP address, with no account or behavioural data. This check exists to prevent proxy-based survey farming, which Survey Partners reject outright; without it, the reward pool shared by all users is what gets charged.

3.6 Access logs

  • Each page request writes a log entry: time, path, IP address, network attribution (country, region, city, carrier and network), referrer domain, browser identifier, and the language preference and request-type header sent by your browser.
  • Logs are used only for traffic statistics and for separating human visits from automated probes. They are visible to the operator only, are not provided to third parties, and are not used for advertising profiles.
  • Logs are retained for 90 days and then deleted automatically. Deleted logs cannot be recovered.

3.7 What we do not collect

  • We do not collect government identifiers, biometric data, precise geolocation, or health information.
  • We do not read contacts, photos or files on your device.
  • We do not run advertising networks, targeted advertising, or cross-site behavioural tracking.
  • We do not use your data to train any model.

4. Lawful basis for processing

Under Article 6 of the GDPR, each processing activity relies on one of the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): registration, sign-in, balance calculation, reward issuance, withdrawals and redemptions. The service cannot be provided without this data.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): preventing duplicate accounts, scripted farming, proxy abuse and account takeover, and keeping access logs for security and troubleshooting. We have balanced these interests; the processing does not override your fundamental rights because you can still use the service normally and the data is limited to what is necessary.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): retention of ledger and withdrawal records to meet financial and tax retention requirements.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): the optional survey profile (age, gender, postal code). You can clear it at any time in Settings; withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that already took place.

5. What we use it for

  • Letting you sign in and see your own balance and records.
  • Calculating and paying rewards correctly, and settling money accurately.
  • Detecting duplicate accounts, scripted activity and false profile data, protecting both Survey Partners and the shared reward pool.
  • Contacting you and verifying details when you submit a ticket or request a withdrawal.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell user profiles.

6. Sharing with third parties

We share only what is necessary to deliver the service, and not for the recipient's own marketing:

  • Survey Partners: when you take a survey, the partner needs to know you are an independent, genuine participant. It receives a participation identifier generated by us, the survey ID, the timestamp, and — only if you chose to provide them — your age, gender and postal code. Your email and password are never shared. Current partners are TheoremReach (a Riwi company), OpinionUniverse and BitLabs.
  • Payment channels: when processing a withdrawal, the payout account and amount are passed to the relevant Alipay, WeChat Pay, PayPal or Payoneer service.
  • Infrastructure: the Site is hosted on Cloudflare, and data is stored in Cloudflare's database and edge network. Transactional email is sent through ZeptoMail.
  • Network lookup: ip-api.com, which receives only an IP address.
  • Legal requirements: where a competent authority issues a valid legal request, within the scope required by law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not disclose it to third parties unrelated to the research service.

6.1 International transfers

Our servers, database and email services are located outside China, primarily in the United States. Information you submit is therefore transferred to and processed in a country other than your own. For data originating in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission as the transfer safeguard, and we require our processors to apply appropriate technical and organisational measures. You may request a description of the safeguards in place by writing to support@chengfa.org.

7. Automated decisions

We apply automated risk checks to prevent abuse: detecting bulk registration from the same network, proxy or datacenter IPs, and abnormal survey click rates. The outcome may be refusal of participation in a specific survey, or, in serious cases, suspension of the account and a deduction of 8 experience points.

  • The inputs are limited to IP network attributes, request frequency, device identifiers and account activity. No sensitive personal data is used.
  • Decisions are never based on race, ethnicity, religion, political opinion, health, sexual orientation or trade-union membership.
  • If you believe a decision was wrong, you may submit a support ticket and request human review. We will respond within 7 business days. You may attach an explanation or evidence.

8. Cookies and local storage

We use a small amount of strictly necessary storage. There are no advertising trackers, and no cookie consent wall is required to use the Site:

  • cf_session: sign-in credential, HttpOnly, valid for 30 days, invalidated on sign-out.
  • cf-currency, cf-cur-style: your chosen display currency and unit format.
  • cf-fx: exchange-rate cache, so the page does not re-request it on every load.
  • cf-me: account summary cached for the current tab, cleared when the tab closes.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile sets its own verification cookie, described under "Security checks" above.

Apart from the session credential, these are display preferences only. Clearing browser data removes them without affecting your account.

9. Retention

  • Account information: until you close your account.
  • Ledger entries, withdrawals and redemptions: retained after account closure to meet financial retention requirements, but de-identified from your account.
  • Sign-in records: normally 180 days, then purged automatically.
  • Access logs: 90 days.
  • Network lookup results: 24 hours.
  • Support tickets and in-site messages: 2 years after resolution, for handling later disputes.

10. Your rights

The following rights can be exercised free of charge, subject to our legal retention obligations:

  • Access: confirm whether we process your data and obtain a copy.
  • Rectification: username, bio and avatar are editable in Settings at any time; other corrections can be requested by email.
  • Erasure (right to be forgotten): request deletion of your account and associated data. Records we are legally required to retain are excluded and will be de-identified.
  • Restriction of processing: where you contest accuracy or object to a processing activity, you may ask us to suspend processing.
  • Data portability: obtain a structured copy of your account data (JSON or CSV).
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests (such as security logging). We will stop unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.
  • Withdrawal of consent: the optional survey profile can be cleared in Settings at any time, without affecting processing that already occurred.
  • Account closure: submit a support ticket requesting closure. We process it within 7 business days; afterwards the account cannot sign in and the profile is no longer public.
  • Complaint: if you believe our processing infringes data protection law, you may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country, or contact us first.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@chengfa.org or open a support ticket. We respond within 30 days. To protect your account, we may ask you to write from your registered email address so we can verify your identity.

11. Notice to California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the CPRA, California residents have the following rights:

  • Right to know: the categories of personal information collected, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of third parties with whom it is shared.
  • Right to delete: request deletion of personal information we collected, subject to legal retention exceptions.
  • Right to correct: request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing: we do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. There is therefore no sale or sharing channel to opt out of. If this changes, we will update this policy and provide a clear opt-out mechanism first.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information: the only sensitive information we collect is account login credentials (email and password hash), used solely for authentication and account security, and not to infer characteristics about you.
  • Right to non-discrimination: exercising these rights will not result in denial of service, different pricing, or a reduced level of service.

Categories collected in the past 12 months: identifiers (email, username, IP address), commercial information (participation records, orders, withdrawals), internet activity (access logs), and account credentials. Sources are you and your browser. Business purposes are described in sections 3 and 5. Recipient categories are described in section 6.

To exercise California rights, email support@chengfa.org or open a support ticket. We respond within 45 days. You may use an authorised agent; we will require written authorisation and may verify your identity directly with you.

12. Children

The Site is intended for users aged 18 and over. Market research generally imposes age requirements, so please do not register if you are under 18. Consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information; under Article 8 of the GDPR, the Site is not directed to children under 16. If an account is found to belong to a minor, we will suspend it and delete the related information. Parents or guardians may request deletion at support@chengfa.org.

13. Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS. The sign-in credential is stored in an HttpOnly cookie that page scripts cannot read.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes and cannot be reversed.
  • Database access is confined to server-side functions; connection credentials are never exposed to the browser.
  • The administration area uses separate authentication and is inaccessible to ordinary users.
  • If a data breach occurs that may affect your rights, we will notify you as soon as reasonably possible through an in-site message or email, describing the scope and the measures taken.

No internet service can promise absolute security. Please use a unique password and do not share your account.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will post a notice on the Site, send an in-site message, and update the date at the top. Where a change expands the purposes or legal bases of processing, we will notify you separately before it takes effect. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated version; if you do not accept it, you may stop using the Site and request account closure.

15. Contact

For any privacy question, data access, correction, deletion or portability request, or to request human review of an automated decision, email support@chengfa.org or open a support ticket. We reply within 7 business days for general enquiries, and within the periods stated above for rights requests.
